# Notices and awards

After a bidding closes, the Philippine Sports Commission publishes what happened to it — who won, when work could start, and whether the process was postponed or cancelled. Each kind of notice has its own page on the PSC website. This page explains what each one means and what the Commission has published on it.

---

## The notices the PSC publishes

| Notice                                               | What it means                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Notice of Pre-bid Conference and Opening of Bids** | Tells bidders when the pre-bid conference and the opening of bids will take place. |
| **Notice of Postponement**                           | The opening of bids has been moved, and the notice covers the re-scheduling.       |
| **Notice of Resumption of Opening of Bids**          | A postponed opening is going ahead on a new date.                                  |
| **Notice of Cancellation**                           | The bidding for a project has been cancelled outright.                             |
| **Notice of Award**                                  | The contract has been awarded to a winning bidder.                                 |
| **Notice to Proceed**                                | The winning supplier may now begin delivery or work.                               |
| **Purchase Order**                                   | The Commission's order for goods or services, issued against an award.             |

Contracts themselves are published separately as **Contract of Agreement** records, and the minutes of each stage — pre-bid conference, opening of bids, negotiation conference — are published as their own documents. See **Bid documents and bulletins**.

---

## Notice of Award

The Commission's Notice of Award page lists awarded procurements by date, each linking to the signed notice. Recent entries cover purchases such as consumables and parts for a copier machine at the Office of the Deputy Executive Director for administrative services, spare parts for the Fargo ID printer used to issue PSC identification cards, electrical works at the Rizal Memorial Badminton Hall, preventive maintenance of the LED displays at the Rizal Memorial Sports Complex and the Philsports Complex, and a refrigerator for specimen storage at the anti-doping office.

The page is where to check whether a procurement you bid on has been decided.

---

## Notice to Proceed

Published in the same form: a date, the procurement it relates to, and a link to the signed notice. The Notice to Proceed is what starts the clock on a supplier's delivery period, so the gap between an award and its notice to proceed is worth checking if you are tracking a contract.

---

## Notice of Cancellation

Cancellations are published with their legal basis stated on the page:

> "Notice is hereby issued CANCELLING the bidding of the following projects pursuant to Section 41 of the Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations of R.A. 9184."

Cancelled biddings have included preventive maintenance for air-conditioning units at the Rizal Memorial Sports Complex and the Philsports Complex (March 2026), swimming pool maintenance supplies (August 2025), specialised luggage for national athletes (July 2025), training uniforms for the national training pool (July 2025), and the supply, delivery and installation of artificial turf at the Rizal Memorial Football and Oval Stadium, cancelled twice in 2024.

---

## Notice of Postponement and Notice of Resumption

Postponements are grouped by year, running back to 2017. Recent entries include the supply, delivery and installation of Microsoft Office 365 E3, and training uniforms for national athletes and coaches for FY2026.

The Notice of Resumption page is thinner and less current: its most recent entries are from December 2023 and November 2023, with earlier entries from 2021. If an opening was postponed and no resumption notice appears, ask the Procurement Office rather than assuming the procurement has lapsed.

---

## Purchase Order

The Purchase Order page lists orders by date with the item or service ordered. Recent entries range from commemorative wristwatches for loyalty awardees on the Commission's 36th anniversary, to plumbing materials for the administrative building at the Rizal Memorial Sports Complex, to an online legal research subscription for the Legal Affairs Office, to electronic equipment for the Bids and Awards Committee office.

---

## What these pages are like to use

A few things are worth knowing before you go looking for a record:

- **Entries are dated but not numbered.** Most notices name the procurement and the date, not the PSCBAC reference, so match on the project title.
- **Dates are not always in order**, and a handful carry obvious typographical errors in the year. The signed document behind the link is the authoritative version.
- **Some pages are barely maintained.** The Notice of Pre-bid Conference page holds a single entry, dated 29 November 2021. The Minutes of the Negotiation page holds one record, for the janitorial services contract procured by negotiation after two failed biddings.
- **The Commission publishes no consolidated list of contracts awarded**, no contractor or supplier register, and no contract values on the notice pages. The amounts are inside the signed documents.

---

## Where to ask

For a copy of a notice, a signed contract or an award that is not published, contact the **Procurement Office** — procurement@psc.gov.ph, local 143 through trunkline 8525-0808 — or file a request through the **Freedom of Information** programme.
