# Bid documents and bulletins

Before bidding on Philippine Sports Commission work, you need the documents that define it: the Philippine Bidding Documents, the Bid Data Sheet and the Terms of Reference, plus any bulletin that has since amended them. This page explains what the Commission publishes, how amendments are numbered, and where the minutes of each stage appear.

---

## The documents the PSC publishes

| Document                                                                  | What it is                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PBD, Bid Data Sheet and Terms of Reference**                            | The Philippine Bidding Documents for a procurement, with the Bid Data Sheet that sets its specific conditions and the Terms of Reference describing what is being bought. |
| **Supplemental / Bid Bulletin**                                           | An amendment or clarification issued after the bidding documents were published.                                                                                          |
| **Republic Act No. 9184**                                                 | The Government Procurement Reform Act, the law the whole process runs under.                                                                                              |
| **Philippine Bidding Documents for Infrastructure Projects, 5th Edition** | The standard document set for works contracts.                                                                                                                            |
| **Contract Agreement**                                                    | The contract signed with the winning bidder.                                                                                                                              |

Minutes are published separately: **Minutes of Pre-Bid Conference**, **Opening of Bids Minutes** and **Minutes of the Negotiation Conference**.

---

## Bidding documents

The Commission's PBD, Bid Data Sheet and Terms of Reference page is organised by year, from 2018 to the current year, with a table for each. Its columns are **Particulars, ABC, Published** and **Opening** — ABC being the Approved Budget for the Contract, and Opening the date bids will be opened, rather than the closing date used on the bidding listings.

Always read the bidding documents together with the listing on the year's bidding page. The listing tells you the deadline; the bidding documents tell you the requirements, the specifications and the terms you would be signing up to.

---

## Supplemental bid bulletins

A supplemental bid bulletin is how the Commission changes a procurement after it has been advertised — correcting a specification, answering a question raised at the pre-bid conference, or adjusting a schedule. Bulletins are numbered against the procurement they amend:

> SUPPLEMENTAL BID BULLETIN NO. 1 (PSCBAC-007-2026)

The page holds bulletins by year from 2018 onwards. **Check for a bulletin before you submit.** A bulletin forms part of the bidding documents, and a bid prepared from the unamended version can be non-responsive.

---

## Minutes

| Minutes                                   | What they cover                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Minutes of Pre-Bid Conference**         | The questions raised and clarifications given before bids were prepared. |
| **Opening of Bids Minutes**               | What was submitted and what happened when the bids were opened.          |
| **Minutes of the Negotiation Conference** | Negotiations where a procurement moved to negotiated mode.               |

The minutes pages are organised by project title rather than by date. Coverage is uneven: the pre-bid conference minutes run back through procurements from 2021 to 2023, the opening of bids minutes hold mostly 2018 and 2019 records, and the negotiation conference page has exactly one entry — the procurement of janitorial services for PSC venues, negotiated after two failed biddings.

---

## Contract agreements

Signed contract agreements are published by date with the subject of the contract. Recent records cover services such as venue and meal hire for anti-doping education sessions ahead of the ASEAN Para Games and Southeast Asian Games, hotel accommodation for the Batang Pinoy athletes' outreach programme, and venue and technical equipment hire for inter-agency work around the 2025 FIVB Volleyball Men's World Championship and the 2025 FIFA Futsal Women's World Cup.

---

## Annual Procurement Plan

The Commission's Annual Procurement Plan, its supplemental and updated versions, and its Procurement Monitoring Reports are not published with the bidding documents. They are filed under the **Transparency Seal**, covering FY2018 to FY2026. If you want to see what the PSC intends to buy before it advertises, that is where to look.

---

## What to check before bidding

1. Find the listing for the procurement on the current year's bidding page and note its **PSCBAC reference and closing date**.
2. Download the **PBD, Bid Data Sheet and Terms of Reference** for the same reference and year.
3. Check the **supplemental bid bulletin** page for any amendment carrying that reference.
4. If a pre-bid conference was held, read the **minutes** — they often contain the clarification you were about to ask for.
5. Direct anything still unclear to the **Procurement Office**, procurement@psc.gov.ph or local 143 through trunkline 8525-0808, before the closing date.

---

## What is not published

The Commission publishes no index linking a procurement's documents, bulletins, minutes and notices together — each sits on its own page, and you match them by project title and reference number. It also publishes no fee or issuance procedure for obtaining bidding documents, and no submission address; those appear only inside the individual documents.
