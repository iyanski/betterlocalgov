# Current bid opportunities

The Philippine Sports Commission publishes every open Request for Quotation and Invitation to Bid on a single page for the current year, grouped by the month it was published. Each listing carries the approved budget, the publication date and the closing date, and links to the document with the actual requirements. This page explains how to read those listings and shows the most recent ones.

---

## Where opportunities are published

|                        |                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Published on**       | The PSC's bidding and procurement page for the current year                                     |
| **Grouped by**         | Month of publication, newest month first                                                        |
| **Each listing shows** | Particulars, Approved Budget, Published date, Closing date                                      |
| **The document**       | A download link on the particulars, holding the full Request for Quotation or Invitation to Bid |

Dates are written in MM-DD-YYYY form and budgets in pesos. The approved budget is the Approved Budget for the Contract — the ceiling above which a bid will not be considered.

**Read the linked document before bidding.** The listing gives you the subject, the ceiling and the deadline; everything else — where to submit, what to submit, who to ask — is inside the Request for Quotation or Invitation to Bid itself.

## Disposal of property

Alongside purchases, the Commission publishes **Invitations to Bid for Disposal** of its own property. These do not carry a single closing date: the listing holds a full schedule instead, covering ocular inspection, the pre-bid conference, the opening of bids, awarding and clearing. Read the schedule in the listing and in the linked document carefully — the dates differ from stage to stage.

## Past years

The Commission keeps a separate page for each procurement year, from 2019 through the current year, plus an older combined bidding page. Two things to know when searching the archive: the 2023 page does not follow the same naming as the others, and the 2019 page is linked from every year's sidebar but has no surviving contents.

For what happened after a bidding closed, see **Notices and awards**. For the documents that govern a bid, see **Bid documents and bulletins**.

## Questions about an open procurement

Contact the **Procurement Office** — procurement@psc.gov.ph, local 143 through trunkline 8525-0808 — and quote the bid reference number, which follows the pattern PSCBAC-<number>-<year>. The Commission publishes no separate Bids and Awards Committee contact.
