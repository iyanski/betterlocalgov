# Board of Commissioners

The Philippine Sports Commission is governed by a Chairman and four Commissioners, all appointed by the President. Together they are known as the Commission Proper, and they are the body that sets national sports policy under Republic Act No. 6847. This page lists the current members, how to reach their offices, and what the law requires of them.

## What the law says about the Commission Proper

**Composition — Section 8.**

> "The Commission shall be composed of a Chairman and four (4) Commissioners all of whom shall be appointed by the President."

**Qualifications — Section 9.**

> "The Chairman and the Commissioners of the Commission must be citizens of the Philippines, publicly-recognized personalities in the field of sports, at least thirty (30) years of age and of good moral character."

**Rank — Section 4.** The Chairman holds "the same level as a department undersecretary" and the Commissioners the level "of department assistant secretaries".

**Compensation — Section 10.** The Chairman is entitled to the compensation, benefits and privileges provided under existing laws for a department undersecretary, and the Commissioners to those of assistant secretaries.

The Commission's full powers and functions are set out on the **Legal mandate (RA 6847)** page.

## What the PSC has not published

- **Biographies of the Chairman and Commissioners.** The Commission publishes no profile, background or message from any member. Its Chairman's Corner page consists of a single photograph with no text.
- **Appointment dates or terms.** The PSC does not publish when each member was appointed or when their terms end.
- **Board resolutions.** The Board Resolution item in the PSC's own navigation menu is a dead link, and no resolutions are published anywhere on the site. Requests for them go to the Office of the Board Secretary, or through the **Freedom of Information** process.
