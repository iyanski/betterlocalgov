# Organizational structure

Below the Chairman and the four Commissioners, the Philippine Sports Commission is run by an Executive Director and split into two bureaus: one for administration and finance, one for programmes and athlete support. This page sets out the whole structure in text, transcribed from the Commission's organizational chart as of June 2026.

---

## The shape of the agency

1. **The Commission Proper** — the Chairman and four Commissioners, who set policy.
2. **The Office of the Executive Director** — runs day-to-day operations, with supervision and control over both bureaus (RA 6847, Sec. 14).
3. **Two bureaus**, each headed by a Deputy Executive Director:
   - **Bureau on Administrative, Financial and Management Services (BAFMS)** — budget, accounting, personnel, procurement, records, property, information systems and transport.
   - **Bureau on Coordinating Secretariat and Support Services (BCSSS)** — programme research and development, assistance and coordination, and sports facilities.
4. **Staff and advisory offices** attached to the Chairman and the Executive Director — the Board Secretary, the Chief-of-Staff, Legal Affairs, communications, the Philippine Sports Institute, the International Games Secretariat, the anti-doping consultancy and the resident Commission on Audit unit.

This two-bureau split is the one written into Section 16 of RA 6847: the administrative bureau covers "financial management, personnel, records, supplies and equipment", and the coordinating secretariat and support services bureau covers "plans and programs formulation, research, EDP and system management, public information and production, transportation, communications".

## About this transcription

The Commission publishes its organizational structure as a single image with no text. The version above is transcribed from the chart captioned **"PSC Organizational Structure — as of June 2026"**, which is the current one on psc.gov.ph.

An older chart captioned "as of March 2026" is still embedded on the PSC's Agency Directory page and differs in three places: Preciosa C. Evangelista was shown as Head of the Program Planning Unit rather than the International Games Secretariat; Prof. Pearl Anne D. Managuelod sat under the Assistance and Coordination Division rather than at Chairman level; and neither the Chief-of-Staff post nor the NST-IAC Secretariat / Sports Tourism Unit appeared at all.

Two things the chart does not tell you:

- **Five boxes carry no name** — the Program Planning Unit, the Monitoring and Evaluation Unit, PSC Cebu, PSC Davao, and the NST-IAC Secretariat / Sports Tourism Unit.
- **The chart has no legend**, so the meaning of its dotted connecting lines — whether they show a reporting line or a coordinating relationship — is not stated. The staff and advisory offices listed under the Chairman and the Executive Director above are the ones joined by those dotted lines.

For local numbers and email addresses for every office named here, see the **Agency directory**.
