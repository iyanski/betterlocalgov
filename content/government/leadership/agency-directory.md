## How to use this directory

- **Local numbers are extensions.** Call a trunkline first, then ask for the local. The number used across the PSC's own site is **8525-0808**; other published trunklines are listed on the **Contact the PSC** page, together with a conflict between two PSC pages that the Commission has not resolved.
- **"Not published" means exactly that.** Those offices have an email address in the Commission's directory but no local extension listed. Reach them by email, or call a trunkline and ask to be transferred.
- **Some entries repeat.** Michelle S. Balunan heads both the Personnel Office (local 150) and the Training and Development Unit (local 190). Local 151 is listed for both the Athlete's Dormitory and the Cashier's Office, and local 190 for both the Engineering and Maintenance Unit and the Training and Development Unit. These duplications are in the Commission's published directory.
- **Names differ slightly between PSC sources.** The directory lists "Engr. Victoria T. Moya" and "Julia G. Llanto", while the June 2026 organizational chart gives "Engr. Maria Victoria T. Moya" and "Julia G. Llanto, J.D.". Both refer to the same officers.
- **The directory carries no revision date.** The Commission does not publish an "as of" date for this table. It was last verified against psc.gov.ph on **17 September 2026**.

For how these offices fit together, see the **Organizational structure** page.
