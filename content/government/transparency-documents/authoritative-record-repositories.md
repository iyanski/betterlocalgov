# Authoritative public-record repositories

Better Gattaran links to the issuing government organizations so residents can consult the current official record.

## Commission on Audit reports

The Commission on Audit publishes annual audit reports for local government units. Use its Local Government Units report collection and search or browse for the Municipality of Gattaran and the relevant audit year.

- [Annual Audit Reports — Local Government Units](https://www.coa.gov.ph/reports/annual-audit-reports/aar-local-government-units/)

## Philippine geographic and population records

The Philippine Statistics Authority publishes the Philippine Standard Geographic Code entry for Gattaran, including administrative codes, income classification, barangays, and population context.

- [PSGC — Municipality of Gattaran](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/barangays/0201513000)

These links do not imply that Better Gattaran is the issuing office. Record availability and repository navigation are controlled by the respective government organization.
