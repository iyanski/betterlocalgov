## How to check for new vacancies

1. **Go to the Careers page on psc.gov.ph.** It is the only place the Commission publishes vacancies, and it is linked from the main menu.
2. **Look at the posting date**, not just the position title. The bulletin is not always current.
3. **Open the publication document** for the position you are interested in. It holds the qualification standards, the requirements and the submission instructions for that vacancy — none of which appear on the page itself.
4. **Watch for extension notices.** The Commission has used the same bulletin to announce extensions of the application period.

## Before you apply

The Commission publishes no application procedure, no document checklist and no recruitment contact alongside its postings. Read the publication document first; if it leaves your question unanswered, contact the office that handles hiring:
