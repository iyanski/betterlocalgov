# Careers at the PSC

The Philippine Sports Commission hires for the offices that run national sports — budget and accounting staff, engineers, lifeguards, physical therapists, venue caretakers, communications staff and dormitory attendants — across its sites in Manila, Pasig and Baguio. This page explains how PSC hiring works, what the Commission publishes, and what it does not.

---

## Types of appointment

| Type                                  | What it means                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Plantilla positions**               | Regular civil service positions, published in the Civil Service Commission's standard vacancy format — for example Accountant III, Administrative Officer V (Budget Officer III), Project Evaluation Officer II, Administrative Aide IV (Driver II). |
| **Contract of Service, marked (COS)** | Contracted posts, used heavily by the Commission — for example Legal Researcher II (COS), Multimedia Artist (COS), Personnel Analyst (COS), Physical Therapist (COS), Venue Caretaker (COS).                                                         |
| **Job order**                         | Short-term hiring tied to an event, such as the job order recruitment run for Batang Pinoy.                                                                                                                                                          |

---

## How vacancies are announced

The Commission posts vacancies on its Careers page, grouped by the date they were posted and tagged by duty station and position title. Each posting links to a publication document — a scanned notice in the Civil Service Commission's vacancy publication format — which is where the requirements for that position are set out.

The Commission also uses the same page for process announcements. It has, for example, posted an extension of the period for submission of job applications rather than issuing it separately.

## What the Commission does not publish

This is the honest position, and it matters if you are planning an application:

- **There is no published application process.** The Careers page gives no instructions on how to apply, where to send documents, or whether applications are accepted by email or only in person.
- **There is no published document checklist.** The Commission names no required forms, personal data sheet, transcript, eligibility certificate or work experience sheet on its website.
- **There is no published deadline convention.** Closing dates are not shown next to postings.
- **There is no recruitment contact on the Careers page.** No email address or phone number appears on it at all.

Everything above is inside the individual publication documents linked from each posting, which are scanned files.

---

## What to do instead

1. **Open the publication document for the specific position.** It carries the qualification standards, the requirements and the submission instructions for that vacancy.
2. **If the document does not answer your question, contact the Personnel Office** at hr@psc.gov.ph or local 150. Name the position title and the posting date in your message.
3. **Check the posting date.** The Commission's Careers page can go months without an update — see **Current job vacancies** for what is currently listed and how recent it is.

---

## Working conditions

The Commission publishes no information about salary grades, benefits, working hours, or what it is like to work there. Compensation for plantilla positions follows the government Salary Standardization Law and the salary grade stated in each vacancy publication. For anything beyond that, ask the Personnel Office.
