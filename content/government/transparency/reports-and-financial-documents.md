# Reports and financial documents

The Philippine Sports Commission publishes its budget, spending and performance records as filings under its Transparency Seal. This page explains what each series contains, which years it covers, and which records the Commission does not publish at all — so you know whether to look for a document or to request it.

---

## What each report series tells you

| Report                              | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Years published  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| **FAR No. 1 — SAAOBDB**             | Statement of Appropriations, Allotment, Obligation, Disbursement and Balances: what the Commission was appropriated, what was released to it, what it committed, what it actually paid out, and what was left | FY2013 to FY2026 |
| **Summary Report on Disbursements** | Total money actually paid out over the period                                                                                                                                                                 | FY2013 to FY2026 |
| **BAR No. 1**                       | Quarterly Physical Report of Operations and the Physical Plan: the targets the Commission set and the output it delivered against them                                                                        | FY2013 to FY2026 |
| **FAR No. 5**                       | Quarterly Report on Revenue and Other Receipts: money coming in, including income from facilities                                                                                                             | FY2013 to FY2026 |
| **Financial Plan**                  | The Commission's own spending plan for the year                                                                                                                                                               | FY2013 to FY2026 |

Reports are filed by fiscal year. For the most recent complete picture of a year, read the FAR No. 1 and the Summary Report on Disbursements together.

## Performance-Based Bonus

The Commission files its Performance-Based Bonus records for **2018 through 2024**. These include the **FY2023 Agency Scorecard**, and for **FY2022** both an **Isolation Letter** and an **Agency Scorecard**. The scorecard is the clearest single view of whether the Commission met its own performance commitments for a year.

---

## Procurement records

Procurement filings sit under their own Transparency Seal heading rather than with the financial reports: **Annual Procurement Plans** and their indicative, supplemental and updated versions, **APP-CSE** and **APP-Non-CSE** forms, **Procurement Monitoring Reports** by semester, **posting certifications** and **Early Procurement Activity certifications**, covering **FY2018 to FY2026**.

Individual contracts, awards and purchase orders are published separately on the Commission's procurement pages. See **Notices and awards**.

---

## What the PSC does not publish

Be aware of the gaps before you go looking:

- **No annual report in narrative form.** The Commission publishes statutory financial and physical reports, not a yearly report telling the story of what it did.
- **No Commission on Audit annual audit report.** The PSC's Transparency Seal index has no heading for COA audit reports and links none, although a resident Commission on Audit unit appears on the PSC organizational chart. COA publishes agency audit reports on its own website, coa.gov.ph.
- **No audited financial statements** as a separate published series.
- **No SALN review and compliance procedure.** That Transparency Seal section is marked "(No Changes)" with nothing attached.
- **No system of ranking delivery units.** Also marked "(No changes)".

## If a document will not open

Many older filings sit on legacy parts of the PSC's website, including addresses under `web.psc.gov.ph` for FY2013 to FY2016 reports, and some of those links no longer work. One link on the live Transparency Seal page is malformed and cannot load at all.

If a document you need is missing or its link is broken, request it through the **Freedom of Information** programme — write to the Records Office at records@psc.gov.ph or file through foi.gov.ph. For a question about a specific figure, the Accounting Division (accounting@psc.gov.ph, local 179 / 180) and the Budget Division (budget@psc.gov.ph, local 177 / 176) are the responsible offices.
