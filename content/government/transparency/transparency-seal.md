# Transparency Seal

The Transparency Seal is the index of documents every national government agency must publish about its mandate, budget, spending, programmes and procurement. This page explains what the Philippine Sports Commission publishes under each of the twelve required headings, how far back each series goes, and which sections are empty.

---

## Why agencies publish this

National Budget Circular 542, issued by the Department of Budget and Management on 29 August 2012, requires compliance with Section 93 of the General Appropriations Act of FY2012 — the Transparency Seal provision:

> "Sec. 93. Transparency Seal. To enhance transparency and enforce accountability, all national government agencies shall maintain a transparency seal on their official websites. The transparency seal shall contain the following information: (i) the agency's mandates and functions, names of its officials with their position and designation, and contact information; (ii) annual reports, as required under National Budget Circular Nos. 507 and 507-A dated January 31, 2007 and June 12, 2007, respectively, for the last three (3) years; (iii) their respective approved budgets and corresponding targets immediately upon approval of this Act; (iv) major programs and projects categorized in accordance with the five key results areas under E.O. No. 43, s. 2011; (v) the program/projects beneficiaries as identified in the applicable special provisions; (vi) status of implementation and program/project evaluation and/or assessment reports; and (vii) annual procurement plan, contracts awarded and the name of contractors suppliers/consultants.
>
> The respective heads of the agencies shall be responsible for ensuring compliance with this section."

The seal itself is a pearl shining out of an open shell. In the Department of Budget and Management's words, "A pearl buried inside a tightly-shut shell is practically worthless. Government information is a pearl, meant to be shared with the public in order to maximize its inherent value."

## II. Annual financial reports

The Commission publishes five separate report series, each filed by fiscal year from FY2013 through FY2026:

- **FAR No. 1 — SAAOBDB**, the Statement of Appropriations, Allotment, Obligation, Disbursement and Balances
- **Summary Report on Disbursements**
- **BAR No. 1** — Quarterly Physical Report of Operations / Physical Plan
- **FAR No. 5** — Quarterly Report on Revenue and Other Receipts
- **Financial Plan**

These are the documents to look at for how much the PSC was given and how much it actually spent. See the **Reports and financial documents** page for what each one contains.

## III to IV. Budget, targets and programmes

- **DBM-approved budget (FY2019–FY2025)** and the corresponding **targets, MFOs and GAA targets** for the same years.
- **Projects and programmes (FY2019–FY2024)**.
- **Status of implementation (FY2015–FY2023)**.
- **Beneficiaries**: the Commission's FY2023 entry reads "not applicable", and no beneficiary lists are published for other years.

---

## V. Annual Procurement Plan

The Commission files a large set of procurement documents here rather than on its bidding pages: the **Annual Procurement Plan** and its indicative, supplemental and updated versions, the **APP-CSE** and **APP-Non-CSE** forms for common-use and non-common-use items, **Procurement Monitoring Reports** by semester, **posting certifications**, and **Early Procurement Activity certifications**. Filings run from FY2018 to FY2026.

Individual bid opportunities are published separately — see **Current bid opportunities**.

---

## VI. Quality management certification

The heading is "QMS Certification of at least one core process by an international certifying body (ICB)". The Commission lists three ISO 9001:2015 certificates, issued September 2024, December 2022 and September 2019, along with attestation certificates for CY2020, CY2021, CY2023 and CY2024 and a Quality Management Manual. Full detail is on the **Citizen's Charter and quality management** page.

---

## X to XI. Service standards and performance

- **Citizen's Charter Handbook** — seven editions, from the 2020 1st Edition through the 2026 1st Edition.
- **Performance-Based Bonus** — filings for 2018 through 2024, including the FY2023 Agency Scorecard, and for FY2022 both an Isolation Letter and an Agency Scorecard.

---

## Sections with nothing published

Three of the twelve headings carry no documents at all:

- **VII. System of Ranking Delivery Units** — marked "(No changes)".
- **VIII. Agency Review and Compliance Procedure of Statements and Financial Disclosure** — marked "(No Changes)". This is the heading under which an agency's **SALN** review and compliance procedure would appear. The PSC links no document here, so its SALN review and compliance procedure is not published on its website.
- **XII. Committee on Anti-Red Tape Act** — the heading appears with nothing beneath it.

## Known problems with the published links

Older entries on the PSC's Transparency Seal point at web addresses that are no longer part of the current site — `web.psc.gov.ph` for 2013 to 2016 reports, and separate `/Transparency Seal/`, `/Career/`, `/QMS/` and `/n4321/` paths for later years. Some of those links no longer resolve, and at least one href on the live page is a malformed double address that cannot load at all. If a document you need does not open, request it through the **Freedom of Information** process.
