# Citizen's Charter and quality management

The Citizen's Charter is the document that tells you what each Philippine Sports Commission service involves — the requirements, the fees, the processing time and the office responsible. The Commission publishes it as a downloadable handbook rather than as web pages, and backs it with an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system. This page explains where to find both and what the Commission has and has not published.

---

## The Citizen's Charter

Every PSC frontline service, together with its requirements, fees, processing times and responsible office, is defined in the **Citizen's Charter Handbook**. Nothing equivalent exists as web pages on psc.gov.ph — if you need the service standard for a specific transaction, the handbook is the only published source.

The Commission has released seven editions:

| Edition     | Year |
| ----------- | ---- |
| 1st Edition | 2026 |
| 1st Edition | 2025 |
| 5th Edition | 2024 |
| 4th Edition | 2023 |
| 3rd Edition | 2022 |
| 2nd Edition | 2021 |
| 1st Edition | 2020 |

All seven are linked from section X of the **Transparency Seal**. Editions are numbered as the Commission numbers them; the count restarts in some years. The 2026 edition is hosted on a different part of the PSC's site from the earlier ones, so a link that worked for an older edition will not simply point at the newest one.

## Quality management system

The Commission's Transparency Seal heading for this is "QMS Certification of at least one core process by an international certifying body (ICB)". What the PSC publishes:

| Item                          | Detail                                                                     |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Standard**                  | ISO 9001:2015                                                              |
| **Certificates**              | Three, issued **September 2024**, **December 2022** and **September 2019** |
| **Attestation certificates**  | CY2020, CY2021, CY2023 and CY2024                                          |
| **Quality Management Manual** | Published                                                                  |
| **Certifying body**           | Not stated anywhere on the PSC website                                     |

The Commission names the standard and the issue dates but never names the body that issued the certificates, and does not publish the certificate numbers, the certified scope in words, or the validity dates. The certificate files themselves are scanned images, so that information cannot be read out of them either. The Commission's own QMS page describes the status of certification as "( Ongoing )".

### Operational procedures

Each office publishes a **Procedure and Work Instruction Manual (PAWIM)** setting out how it does its work. Manuals exist for:

- **Commission Proper and staff offices** — Office of the Chairman, Board Secretary Office, Public Communications Office, Office of the Executive Director, Legal Affairs Office, BAC Secretary Office
- **Bureau on Administrative, Financial and Management Services** — Budget Division, Accounting Division, Management Services Division, Personnel Section, Training and Development Unit, Procurement Office, Property and Supplies Unit, Records Office, Cashier's Office, Information Systems Unit
- **Bureau on Coordinating Secretariat and Support Services** — Program Research and Development Division, Assistance and Coordination Division, NSA Affairs Office, Sea and Air Transportation Unit, Medical and Scientific Athletes Services, Motorpool, Sports Facilities Division, Engineering and Maintenance Section

These are published as "uncontrolled copy" PDFs. They describe internal procedure, not the service standards you would use as a member of the public — for those, use the Citizen's Charter.

## Anti-red tape

The Commission's Transparency Seal carries a heading for the **Committee on Anti-Red Tape Act** with nothing published under it. No committee membership, contact point or report is available on the website. Concerns about a PSC transaction can be raised with the office that handled it — see the **Agency directory** — or through the **Freedom of Information** process.
