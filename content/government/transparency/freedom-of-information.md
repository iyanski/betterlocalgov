# Freedom of Information

Anyone may ask the Philippine Sports Commission for a copy of its records under the government's Freedom of Information programme. You can file online through the eFOI portal or submit a request form at the PSC Records Office. This page sets out both routes, who receives your request, how long the Commission has to answer, and what to do if you are not satisfied.

## How to file through eFOI

1. Go to **www.foi.gov.ph** in your browser.
2. Click **Sign Up** and fill in all the required fields. Attach a valid ID to create an account.
3. Once logged in, you will land on your **Dashboard**, which holds all the FOI requests made from your account.
4. Click **Make a Request**, then select the agency you want to ask — the Philippine Sports Commission.
5. On the **Make a Request** page, fill in all the fields and click **Send My Request**.
6. The agency evaluates your request and notifies you **within 15 working days**.
7. The agency prepares the information for release in the format you asked for, and sends it to you according to your stated preference.

## If you are not satisfied with the answer

You may ask the Commission to carry out an internal review of its response. In the PSC's own words:

> "If you are not satisfied with the response to your FOI request, you may ask us to carry out an internal review of the response by writing to records@psc.gov.ph. Your review request should explain why you are dissatisfied with the response, and should be made within 15 calendar days from the date when you received this letter. We will complete the review and tell you the result within 30 working days from the date when we received your appeal."

| Step                                                     | Deadline                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Commission notifies you of its decision on your request  | Within 15 working days                            |
| You file an appeal for internal review                   | Within 15 calendar days of receiving the response |
| Commission completes the review and tells you the result | Within 30 working days of receiving your appeal   |

Your appeal must say why you are dissatisfied with the response, and it goes to the same address: **records@psc.gov.ph**.

## What the PSC publishes about its FOI programme

The Commission posts four items under Freedom of Information:

- **One Page Manual**, in English and Tagalog — the summary this page is drawn from
- **FOI Manual** — the full manual
- **FOI Reports** — the Commission's periodic reports on requests received and acted on
- **Certificate of Compliance**

Two caveats about those files. The Tagalog one-page manual is named on the Commission's page but no working link to it could be found. And the full FOI Manual is published as a scanned document, so its text cannot be searched or copied — the one-page manual is the usable version.

The Commission's tagline for the programme: **"Be informed. Be engaged. Know your government better."**

## What to ask the PSC for

The Commission publishes a great deal as documents rather than as web pages, and several of those documents are scans. FOI is the route to records that are not online at all, including:

- Board resolutions of the Commission Proper — the Commission publishes none, and the Board Resolution link in its own menu is dead
- Its SALN review and compliance procedure, which its Transparency Seal marks as "(No Changes)" with nothing attached
- Bid documents, minutes and awards older than those still posted on the procurement pages
- Any Transparency Seal document whose link has broken

Before filing, check the **Transparency Seal** index — much of the Commission's financial, procurement and performance record is already published there.
