# Contact the PSC

The Philippine Sports Commission works out of the Rizal Memorial Sports Complex in Malate, Manila. Calls go through a trunkline and are transferred to a local extension, and every office has its own email address — the Commission does not publish a single general enquiry address, so this page shows you which office to reach for what.

**8525-0808 · 8524-4408 · 8523-6055 · 8525-2075 · 8521-2364 · 8523-9831 · 8400-1864**

Two PSC pages publish trunkline lists that do not match each other. The Agency Directory page lists **8525-0808, 8524-4336, 8525-2240, 8523-9831, 8524-6055, 8524-4408, 8400-1864** instead. Once you are connected, ask for the local extension of the office you need. Local numbers are on the **Agency directory** page.

## What the PSC has not published

- **A general public email address.** Only per-office addresses exist. Treat any `info@` or `inquiry@` address claiming to be the PSC's with suspicion.
- **Office hours.** The Commission publishes no opening hours for its head office or for any individual office on its website.
- **Direct-dial numbers.** Only trunklines and local extensions are published; no office has a published direct line or mobile number.
