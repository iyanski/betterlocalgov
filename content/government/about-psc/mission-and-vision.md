# Mission and vision

The Philippine Sports Commission states its purpose in three parts: a vision of what Philippine sports should become, a mission describing how the Commission works towards it, and two basic objectives that everything else is measured against. All three are reproduced here exactly as the Commission publishes them.

---

## Vision

> A Commission with a unified sports program which will enhance the quality of life of the Filipinos, instill national pride and attain international prestige through excellence in sports.

---

## Mission

> To serve as the prime catalyst and advocate for the propagation and development of Philippine sports by helping shape policies and setting priorities through the following:
>
> - coordinating and implementing a national sports program;
> - creating equitable opportunities for participation in sports by all sectors;
> - providing assistance to stakeholders and partners;
> - supporting the specially talented athletes for high level competitions; and
> - promoting the development of those physical qualities and moral values which is the basis of sports.

---

## The two basic objectives

> In view of the foregoing guiding principles, vision and mission statement, the PSC will pursue two basic objectives:
>
> - Increased participation in sports by Filipinos; and
> - Excellence in sports performance by Filipinos.

These same two objectives are also printed under the vision statement on the Commission's own Mission and Vision page. That repetition is in the source; it is not an error in this page.

---

## How this connects to the law

The mission and vision restate, in the Commission's own words, the objectives written into **Republic Act No. 6847, Section 6**:

> "(a) To provide the leadership, formulate the policies and set the priorities and direction of all national amateur sports promotion and development, particularly giving emphasis on grass-roots participation;
> (b) To encourage wide participation of all sectors, government and private, in amateur sports promotion and development; and
> (c) To supplement government appropriations for sports promotion and development."

The full statutory text, including the Commission's fourteen functions and its powers, is on the **Legal mandate (RA 6847)** page.

---

## What the Commission has not published

The PSC publishes no strategic plan, performance targets or key result areas in narrative form on its website. Its yearly targets are filed as documents instead — the DBM-approved budget and corresponding targets, and the physical reports of operations, are listed under the **Transparency Seal**, and the service standards it commits to are set out in the **Citizen's Charter**.
