# Gintong Gawad Awards

The Gintong Gawad Awards, or GiGa Awards, were created as a tribute to grassroots women in sports. The Philippine Sports Commission ran the awards in 2021 and 2022, honouring Filipina athletes, coaches, leaders and community organisers across eight categories.

---

## What the awards recognise

The Gintong Gawad Awards honour the contributions of Filipinas, athletes and coaches alike, in promoting sport at the grassroots level. They were the brainchild of former PSC Commissioner **Celia Kiram**.

|                                     |                                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **First edition**                   | 2021                                                |
| **Second edition**                  | 2022                                                |
| **Categories in the debut edition** | Eight                                               |
| **Entries in the debut edition**    | 200 entries from 38 provinces and cities nationwide |
| **Created by**                      | Office of then PSC Commissioner Celia Kiram         |

## The eight award categories

| Category                                                              | In English                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ina ng Isport                                                         | Mother of sport                                                           |
| Babaeng Atleta, Modelo ng Kabataan                                    | Woman athlete, model for the youth                                        |
| Babaeng Atletang may Kapansanan, Modelo ng Kabataan na may Kapansanan | Woman athlete with a disability, model for young people with disabilities |
| Babaeng Tagasanay ng Isport                                           | Woman sports coach                                                        |
| Babaeng Lider ng Isport                                               | Woman sports leader                                                       |
| Kaagapay ng Isports sa Komunidad                                      | Partner of sport in the community                                         |
| Natatangi at Makabagong Produktong Pang-Isport                        | Outstanding and innovative sports product                                 |
| Proyektong Isport Pang-Kababaihan                                     | Sports project for women                                                  |

The English column is a plain reading of the Filipino category names, provided for readers who need it. The PSC publishes the categories in Filipino only.

## How to nominate someone

**The PSC publishes no nomination form, criteria, deadline, or entry procedure for the Gintong Gawad Awards.** It also does not say whether the awards are still being held. The most recent PSC material on them dates from March 2024 and describes the 2021 and 2022 editions in the past tense.

| Information                            | Status                      |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Whether the awards are still running   | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Nomination criteria and form           | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Nomination deadline                    | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Who judges the entries                 | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
