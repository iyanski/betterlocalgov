# Philippine Sports Hall of Fame

The Philippine Sports Hall of Fame is the country's highest sporting honour, administered by the Philippine Sports Commission under Republic Act No. 8757. Enshrinements have been held in 2010, 2016, 2018 and 2021, and the PSC issues a call for nominations with a criteria and nomination form.

---

## The basics

|                                  |                                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Legal basis**                  | Republic Act No. 8757, cited by the PSC as the law behind the Hall of Fame                                                   |
| **Administered by**              | Philippine Sports Commission                                                                                                 |
| **Enshrinement years on record** | 2010 (first), 2016 (second), 2018, 2021 (fourth)                                                                             |
| **Nominations**                  | The PSC publishes a call for nominations, an invitation letter, and a criteria and nomination form, all as downloadable PDFs |

---

## How to nominate someone

The PSC publishes three documents for nominators:

1. **Call for Nominations**
2. **Invitation Letter**
3. **Criteria for Nomination and Nomination Form**

All three are PDFs on the PSC website. **The criteria themselves, the eligibility rules, the closing date, and the address for submitting a completed form are inside those PDFs. None of it appears on the web page.** Download the current criteria and nomination form before preparing a nomination, and check the deadline on the form itself.

The PSC does not name on its website the office that receives nominations. If the form does not say, ask the Office of the Board Secretary (8525-0808 local 169, boardsecretary@psc.gov.ph) or the Integrated Communication and Events Office (8525-0808 local 340, ice@psc.gov.ph).
