# Women in Sports Awards

The Philippine Sports Commission runs one awards programme dedicated to women in sport: the Gintong Gawad Awards. This page explains what exists, what the PSC's Women in Sports programme covers, and what the PSC does not award.

---

## The one dedicated awards programme

**The [Gintong Gawad Awards](/services/awards-and-recognition/gintong-gawad-awards)** are the PSC's awards for women in sport. They honour Filipina athletes, coaches, leaders and community organisers at the grassroots level, across eight categories, and were held in 2021 and 2022.

That page carries the categories, the first batch of awardees, and what the PSC does not publish about nominations.

---

## What else the Women in Sports programme includes

The PSC's Women in Sports programme has three published components. Two of them are activities rather than awards.

| Component                                                                         | What it is                                                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[Gintong Gawad Awards](/services/awards-and-recognition/gintong-gawad-awards)** | Awards for grassroots women in sport                                                                                                        |
| **Women's Martial Arts Festival**                                                 | An annual competition festival for Filipina martial artists, inaugurated April 2014. Over 15,000 women and girls have taken part since 2017 |
| **[Rise Up! Shape Up!](/services/grassroots-sports/rise-up-shape-up)**            | A web series for women and girls launched in October 2020                                                                                   |

See [Women in sports](/services/inclusive-sports/women-in-sports) for the programme as a whole.

## What the PSC does not award

It is worth stating clearly, because these are the honours people often look for:

| Award                                                                          | Status                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A national "Female Athlete of the Year" award                                  | Not awarded by the PSC. The Philippine Sportswriters Association has historically given athlete of the year honours, and several PSC Hall of Fame profiles cite PSA awards |
| A women's sports leadership or administrator award separate from Gintong Gawad | Not published on psc.gov.ph                                                                                                                                                |
| A separate women's coaching award separate from the Gintong Gawad category     | Not published on psc.gov.ph                                                                                                                                                |
| Any awards programme for women in sport currently open for nominations         | Not published on psc.gov.ph                                                                                                                                                |

The Philippine Sports Hall of Fame is not women-specific, but Filipina athletes are among its inductees, including Haydée Coloso-Espino, Lydia de Vega, Arianne Cerdeña, Elma Muros-Posadas and Gertrudes Lozada. See [Philippine Sports Hall of Fame](/services/awards-and-recognition/philippine-sports-hall-of-fame).

---

## Oversight

The Women in Sports portfolio has had a designated oversight commissioner. Celia Kiram held it when Rise Up! Shape Up! and the Gintong Gawad Awards were created, and Commissioner **Olivia "Bong" Coo** is named as the women in sports oversight commissioner in the PSC's 2024 material.

**The PSC does not publish a current list of commissioner oversight assignments**, so confirm this before relying on it.
