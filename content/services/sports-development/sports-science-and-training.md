# Sports science and training

The Philippine Sports Commission has three units with a sports science, medical and research remit: Medical and Scientific Athletes' Services, the Philippine Sports Institute, and the Program, Research and Development Division. None of them publishes a service catalogue, so this page tells you who they are and what to ask them.

## Anti-doping

Anti-doping for Philippine sport sits with the **Philippine National Anti-Doping Organization (PNADO)**. The PSC's organisational structure names **Dr. Alejandro Pineda** as Head Consultant for PNADO.

**The PSC does not publish anti-doping rules, the prohibited list, testing procedures, therapeutic use exemption forms, or results management procedures.** Athletes and NSAs should get these from PNADO and from the World Anti-Doping Agency's code, not from this site.

---

## The research mandate

Republic Act No. 6847 gives the PSC an explicit research function.

- **Section 7(k)**: conduct basic and applied research on sports development.
- **Section 7(f)**: plan and formulate programmes, and review or evaluate the Commission's organisational set-up, projects and programmes from time to time.
- **Section 7(h)**: assist the proper government agency in formulating an industry incentives programme for the manufacture in the Philippines of sports equipment and supplies of international standard.

## What the PSC does not publish

This is a large gap, and it affects athletes and coaches directly.

| Information                                                                                            | Status                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| What sports science services are offered, and to whom                                                  | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| How a national athlete books a medical or sports science consultation                                  | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Whether physiological testing, strength and conditioning, nutrition or psychology support is available | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Clinic hours and location details for Medical and Scientific Athletes' Services                        | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Whether NSAs outside the national pool can access these services                                       | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Research outputs, studies or data produced under Section 7(k)                                          | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Anti-doping rules, testing or education materials                                                      | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Philippine Sports Institute course list, calendar or entry requirements                                | Not published on psc.gov.ph |

If you are a national athlete or a national sports association that needs any of these services, contact the offices above directly and ask what is available for your sport. Do not assume a service exists because a unit with a matching name exists.
