# Seminars and capability building

The Philippine Sports Commission runs training and education for athletes, coaches, national sports associations and local sports councils. It does not publish a training calendar, so this page lists the programmes that are on record and who to ask about the next one.

---

**The Philippine Sports Institute publishes no course list, curriculum, calendar, fee schedule, or entry requirements on psc.gov.ph.** If you want to enrol in a course or ask whether one exists for your sport, email the institute directly.

The PSC also has an internal **Training and Development Unit** (Michelle S. Balunan, 8525-0808 local 190, trainings@psc.gov.ph). That unit's published remit is staff development inside the Commission, not public courses.

## Programmes on record

### Safe Sport Workshop for National Sports Associations

Held 19 and 20 April 2024, an initiative of Commissioner Olivia "Bong" Coo. Every NSA, including sports under the Philippine Paralympic Committee, was invited to send a board member or official, a coach and an athlete. About 40 NSAs registered. The aim was to help each association build its own safeguarding system.

See [Safe sports and athlete protection](/services/sports-development/safe-sports-and-athlete-protection).

### Financial literacy seminars for national athletes and coaches

The PSC has run these three times.

| Edition | Year           | Notes                                                                                                                 |
| ------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First   | 2015           | With registered financial planner and journalist Salve Duplito                                                        |
| Second  | 2019           | With Salve Duplito                                                                                                    |
| Third   | September 2023 | "Pera Mo, Kinabukasan Mo!", for around 400 national training pool athletes and coaches at the Rizal Memorial Coliseum |

The 2023 edition was overseen by Commissioner Walter Torres and brought in speakers from Landbank of the Philippines and the Overseas Filipino Bank, Pioneer Life, Scam Watch Pilipinas, Pag-IBIG, TESDA, and financial educator Chinkee Tan. Commissioner Torres framed it around the cash incentives paid after the 2023 SEA Games and ASEAN Para Games: "We felt the need to also guide them to take care of their hard-earned peso."

## Capability building for local governments and sports councils

Republic Act No. 6847 gives the PSC a standing job in building local sports capacity.

- **Section 7(m)**: encourage, promote and sustain the creation and establishment of regional, provincial, municipal and barangay or school district sports promotion and development councils. These councils are composed of education and local government officials and private sector representatives, and they initiate, conduct and coordinate sports activities in their own areas.
- **Section 7(j)**: recommend to national government agencies with sports programmes that they carry a separate and specific sports budget line.
- **Section 7(b)**: establish and maintain linkages with international sports associations and national sports bodies of other countries.

**The PSC publishes no guide, toolkit, or model ordinance for setting up a local sports council**, which is the single most useful thing it could give a local government under this section.
