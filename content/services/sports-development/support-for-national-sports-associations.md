# Support for national sports associations

National sports associations run their own sports. The Philippine Sports Commission recognises them, funds and assists them, and supervises the programmes it pays for. This page sets out the division of responsibility under Republic Act No. 6847 and how an NSA reaches the PSC.

---

## Who does what

|                                                                  | Philippine Sports Commission                                            | National sports association                                     | Philippine Olympic Committee                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Status**                                                       | Sole policy-making and coordinating body for amateur sports development | Autonomous, with exclusive technical control over its own sport | Autonomous, recognised as the National Olympic Committee |
| **Selects national teams**                                       | No                                                                      | Yes                                                             | No                                                       |
| **Sets the training programme**                                  | No                                                                      | Yes, in coordination with the POC                               | Coordinates                                              |
| **Funds and assists**                                            | Yes                                                                     | Raises its own funds as well                                    |                                                          |
| **Sends the country to the Olympics, Asian Games and SEA Games** | No                                                                      |                                                                 | Yes, primary responsibility                              |

The PSC's recognition of an NSA is set out in Section 13 of RA 6847: associations organised for their sport in the Philippines, or affiliated with the international federation recognised by the International Olympic Committee, are recognised as such by the Commission.

---

## What an NSA is responsible for

Republic Act No. 6847, Section 13, lists the functions the PSC recognises in NSAs:

- Adopt a constitution and by-laws not inconsistent with those of the Philippine Olympic Committee
- Raise funds by donations, benefits and other means
- Purchase, sell, lease or otherwise encumber property for their purposes
- Conduct competitions to promote their sport
- Appoint their representative to the Philippine Olympic Committee
- Decide all questions on the amateur status and discipline of their athletes and officials, and all disputes between members
- Adopt a training programme for athlete development and preparation for international competition, in coordination with the POC
- Select the athletes, coaches and other officials for the national team, weighing discipline, moral character, aptitude and attitude alongside athletic ability
- Keep accurate records of all official marks, scores and results, confirm them, and furnish copies to the POC
- Qualify and license referees, umpires and other game officials

### The membership rule

No team, school, club, organisation or entity may be admitted as a **voting member** of an NSA unless **60 percent of its athletes are Filipino citizens**. This is a statutory rule, not an internal policy.

---

## What the PSC can do for an NSA

Under Section 11 of RA 6847, the Commission has the power to:

- **Section 11(g)**: assist and support national associations in implementing Section 13
- **Section 11(i)**: confer, extend and grant support or assistance to sports associations in good standing with the Commission
- **Section 11(h)**: grant awards, benefits and privileges to athletes, coaches and officials for outstanding performances

Section 19 also exempts from customs duties and taxes the importation of sportswear, equipment, supplies and materials donated to NSAs through the Commission, where the PSC certifies under oath that they are required for sports development. Section 20 makes donations to NSAs certified by the Commission exempt from donor's and estate taxes and fully deductible for the donor.

---

## Supervision, and the conditions attached

Support comes with oversight. Two provisions matter:

- **Section 11(j)**: the PSC exercises supervisory and visitorial powers over NSAs in connection with the sports programmes for which it extends financial assistance.
- **Section 11(m)**: the PSC may impose sanctions on any national sports association, institution, body, entity, team, athlete or sports official for violation of its policies, rules and regulations.

"Good standing with the Commission" is the phrase that governs whether assistance flows. **The PSC does not publish the criteria for good standing, a list of NSAs currently in good standing, or the procedure and grounds for a sanction.**
