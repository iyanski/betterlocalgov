# Athletes' dormitory

The Philippine Sports Commission houses national athletes in training at the athletes' dormitory inside the Rizal Memorial Sports Complex in Malate, Manila, with further dormitory accommodation at the PSC Baguio facility. A new seven-storey dormitory and training building at the Manila complex began construction in September 2024.

## The new RMSC dormitory

Construction of a seven-storey athletes' dormitory and training facility inside the Rizal Memorial Sports Complex began with a groundbreaking ceremony on **12 September 2024**. It replaces the former boxing and pencak silat training site on that spot.

|                                              |                                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Size**                                     | Seven storeys                                                            |
| **Capacity**                                 | Will serve more than a hundred athletes                                  |
| **Also includes**                            | Training gyms for boxing and pencak silat                                |
| **Championed by**                            | Senator Pia Cayetano                                                     |
| **Construction oversight at groundbreaking** | Department of Public Works and Highways, District Engineer Manny Bulusan |

Athletes from the national training pool in baseball, softball, sepak takraw, indoor hockey and weightlifting attended the groundbreaking.

**The PSC publishes no completion date, construction progress update, or budget figure for the building.** Do not assume it is finished.

## How to get a dormitory place

**The PSC publishes no application procedure, eligibility rule, room rate, house rule, or waiting list for the athletes' dormitory.**

The dormitory is for national athletes in training. In practice, accommodation is arranged through an athlete's national sports association as part of a training camp or national pool assignment, rather than by individual application. To confirm how it works for your sport, contact the dormitory office and your NSA.
