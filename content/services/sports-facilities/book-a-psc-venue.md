# Book a PSC venue

Schools, sports organisations, local governments and private groups can rent Philippine Sports Commission venues. There is no online booking system and no published booking procedure, so every request goes through the Sports Facilities Division by phone or in person. This page tells you exactly what is and is not published.

## Rental rates

The PSC publishes a venue rates document, linked from its facilities page as "View Rental Rates" and issued as a downloadable PDF.

**The rates are inside that PDF only. They are not shown on any web page**, and this site does not reproduce them because a rate copied from a dated PDF goes stale without warning. Ask the Sports Facilities Division for the current rate sheet when you enquire.

One thing the law does settle: under **Section 18 of Republic Act No. 6847**, no tax of any kind may be levied on the use of sports facilities, whether on the user, the owner or the operator. Rental is not taxed.

## What is not published

The PSC publishes no documented booking process. Specifically:

| Information                                              | Status         |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| An online booking or enquiry form                        | Does not exist |
| A written step-by-step booking procedure                 | Not published  |
| A reservation form or letter template                    | Not published  |
| How far in advance to book                               | Not published  |
| Deposit, down payment or cancellation terms              | Not published  |
| Insurance or liability requirements for hirers           | Not published  |
| A venue availability calendar                            | Not published  |
| Whether discounted rates exist for schools, LGUs or NSAs | Not published  |

This gap is worth knowing before you plan an event. Assume you will need to call, ask for the rate sheet, put your request in a letter, and agree terms directly with the division.

## What to have ready when you call

The PSC does not publish a requirements list, so this is practical preparation rather than an official checklist:

1. **The venue and the dates**, including set-up and pack-up days, not just the event day
2. **What the event is**, who is organising it, and expected attendance
3. **Your organisation's details**, and who will sign on its behalf
4. **A contact person** who can answer follow-up questions quickly
