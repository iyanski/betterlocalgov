# Rizal Memorial Sports Complex

The Rizal Memorial Sports Complex in Malate, Manila is the headquarters of the Philippine Sports Commission and the country's oldest major sports venue. Inaugurated in 1934, it is both a working national training site and a declared National Historical Landmark.

## History

The complex was inaugurated in **1934**, in time for the Far Eastern Championship Games. The Art Deco facility was designed by architect **Juan Arellano** and stands on land donated by the **Vito Cruz family**.

It has carried more than sport. During the Philippine Commonwealth the complex was used as a civic area, and it was the site where Manuel Quezon was named one of the presidential candidates in the 1935 elections.

| Year | Milestone                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1934 | Inaugurated for the Far Eastern Championship Games                                                                                                                             |
| 1954 | Reconstructed for the 1954 Asian Games, after destruction in the Second World War                                                                                              |
| 2011 | Renovated                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 2017 | Declared a National Historical Landmark by the National Historical Commission of the Philippines, and an Important Cultural Property by the National Museum of the Philippines |

The 2017 declarations mean the site is protected under the National Cultural Heritage Act.

Republic Act No. 6847, Section 23, is the statute that placed the complex under PSC control: the management and full control of all the land, buildings, equipment and improvements of the Rizal Memorial Sports Complex were transferred to the Commission when the PSC was created.

## Using the complex

Rental rates for PSC venues are published by the PSC as a downloadable rates document. **No online booking form or written booking procedure is published.** See [Book a PSC venue](/services/sports-facilities/book-a-psc-venue) for what is known about the process and who to call.

Note that under Section 18 of RA 6847, no tax of any kind may be levied on the use of sports facilities, whether on the user, the owner or the operator.
