# Philippine Sports Training Center

The Philippine Sports Training Center is a national training complex planned for Bagac, Bataan, on around 250,000 square metres of land donated by the Provincial Government of Bataan. Its construction was institutionalised by Republic Act No. 11214.

---

## The facts

|                               |                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Legal basis**               | Republic Act No. 11214, the Philippine Sports Training Center Act, approved 14 February 2019             |
| **Location**                  | Municipality of Bagac, Bataan                                                                            |
| **Land**                      | Six lots, more or less 250,000 square metres                                                             |
| **How the land was acquired** | Deed of land donation signed by the Philippine Sports Commission and the Provincial Government of Bataan |

---

## What it is meant to do

The PSC states four purposes for the centre:

1. To promote and develop sports in the country
2. To achieve excellence in international sports competitions
3. To ensure success in the country's quest to achieve competitiveness in the Olympic Games
4. To promote international amity among nations

The PSC describes it as a modern sports hub and a training ground intended to develop up-and-coming national athletes to world-class standard.

## The statutory background

Republic Act No. 6847, the PSC Act, already required the PSC to establish training centres. Section 7(d) directs the Commission to "establish, develop and maintain fully-equipped sports facilities and centers in strategic places in the country and, as far as practicable, such modern sports complexes adequate for major international competitions."

Section 22 allows the President, on the recommendation of the Secretary of Environment and Natural Resources, to grant public domain land to the Commission for regional training centres. In Bataan's case the land came by donation from the provincial government rather than by presidential grant.
