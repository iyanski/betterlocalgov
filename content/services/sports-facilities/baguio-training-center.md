# PSC Baguio Training Center

The PSC Baguio facility is the Philippine Sports Commission's high-altitude training site, set up for national combat sports teams. Baguio's cool weather and elevation are the reason the PSC puts its fighters there.

---

## What it is for

The renovated PSC facility in Baguio is geared to accommodate the training of national athletes in combat sports:

- Boxing
- Taekwondo
- Karate
- Muay thai
- Kickboxing
- Kurash

The PSC's stated reason is the climate. The cold weather together with the altitude makes Baguio a strong training ground for combat sports.

Baguio has a long history in Philippine high-performance sport. Project: Gintong Alay, the 1979 national training programme that preceded the PSC, sited its training camp in Baguio City, the first camp of its kind in Southeast Asia.

## Who can train there

**The PSC publishes no access policy, booking procedure, or rates for the Baguio facility.** The site is described as a national athlete training centre rather than a public venue.

If you are a national sports association or a national team asking about training camp access, contact the site office and the Sports Facilities Division.
