# Official hazard and weather information tools

Links to hazard and weather information published by Philippine government agencies. Better Gattaran does not produce its own hazard assessments; use these official tools for the specific location you are concerned about.

## HazardHunterPH

HazardHunterPH is operated by the Department of Science and Technology's Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (DOST-PHIVOLCS) as a product of GeoRisk Philippines. The site describes itself as "the country's one-stop shop for hazard assessment."

- [HazardHunterPH](https://hazardhunter.georisk.gov.ph/)

## PAGASA advisories and bulletins

The Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration (PAGASA) lists these official pages on its home page:

- [Weather Advisory](https://pagasa.dost.gov.ph/weather/weather-advisory)
- [Tropical Cyclone Bulletin](https://pagasa.dost.gov.ph/tropical-cyclone/severe-weather-bulletin)
- [Tropical Cyclone Advisory](https://pagasa.dost.gov.ph/tropical-cyclone-advisory-iframe)
- [Forecast Storm Surge](https://pagasa.dost.gov.ph/tropical-cyclone/forecast-storm-surge)
- [Flood Information](https://pagasa.dost.gov.ph/flood#flood-information)

The weather card on the Better Gattaran home page is not an official warning. For official warnings, use PAGASA.

## What is not published here

Better Gattaran has not located Gattaran-specific hazard statements published by these agencies, and publishes none. Local hotlines are on the [emergency hotlines page](/hotlines).
