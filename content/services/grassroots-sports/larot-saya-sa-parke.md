# Laro't Saya sa Parke

Laro't Saya sa Parke is listed by the Philippine Sports Commission under its Grassroots Program, but the PSC publishes no description, schedule, or participation details for it. This page records what is known and where to ask.

---

## What the PSC publishes

Laro't Saya sa Parke appears in the PSC's Grassroots Program navigation alongside Batang Pinoy, the Philippine National Games and the Indigenous Peoples Games. **The page behind that link has no content.** There is no programme description, no list of participating parks or cities, no schedule, and no way to sign up published anywhere on psc.gov.ph.

The name translates roughly as "play and fun in the park", which suggests community play sessions in public open spaces. That is an inference from the title, not a published fact, and this page will not describe activities the PSC has not described.

---

## The statutory basis

Republic Act No. 6847 gives the PSC an explicit grassroots and community mandate.

- **Section 6(a)** makes it a PSC objective to set the direction of national amateur sports promotion and development, "particularly giving emphasis on grass-roots participation".
- **Section 7(m)** directs the PSC to encourage, promote and sustain the creation of regional, provincial, municipal and barangay or school district sports promotion and development councils, which initiate and coordinate sports activities in their own areas.

A park-based community play programme fits that mandate. The operational detail is simply not on the website.
