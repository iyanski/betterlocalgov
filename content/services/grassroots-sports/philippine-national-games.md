# Philippine National Games

The Philippine National Games is listed by the Philippine Sports Commission as part of its Grassroots Program, but the PSC currently publishes no description, schedule, or entry information for it. This page sets out what is known and where to ask.

---

## What the PSC publishes

The Philippine National Games appears in the PSC's own Grassroots Program navigation alongside Batang Pinoy, the Indigenous Peoples Games and Laro't Saya sa Parke. **The page behind that link has no content.** There is no description of the games, no list of sports, no host city, no calendar and no entry procedure anywhere on psc.gov.ph.

Rather than guess, this page states the gap plainly. Anything you read elsewhere claiming a current PSC national games schedule is not sourced from the PSC website.

---

## The statutory basis

Republic Act No. 6847, the Philippine Sports Commission Act, is the law that makes national games a PSC responsibility. Section 26 constitutes the **National Sports Development Fund** and directs it towards financing:

> the country's integrated sports development program, including the holding of the national games and all other sports competitions at all levels throughout the country as well as the country's participation at international sports competitions.

Section 26 also requires that at least 80 percent of the PSC's annual appropriation, and all of the National Sports Development Fund, be spent on the national sports programme, particularly on the identification, recruitment and training of athletes for pre-regional, regional, national and international competitions.

So a national games sits squarely inside the PSC's funded mandate. What is missing is the published operational detail.
