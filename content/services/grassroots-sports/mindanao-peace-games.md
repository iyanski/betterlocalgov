# Mindanao Peace Games

The Mindanao Peace Games is a regional collegiate sports festival for women student-athletes across Mindanao, held under the tagline "Kalaro. Kaibigan. Kasama." It is organised by a group of Mindanao universities, and the Philippine Sports Commission supports it on request rather than running it.

---

## Who runs it

This is an important distinction if you want to take part. The Mindanao Peace Games is convened by universities in Mindanao, not by the PSC. The PSC provided support to the games through the Office of Commissioner Olivia "Bong" Coo after the organisers requested it.

That means entries, dates and hosting are decided by the convenors, and PSC support follows a request from them.

---

## The 2024 edition

The games returned to the annual sports calendar in June 2024, hosted by Iligan City at the Iligan Medical Center College covered court.

|                  |                                                     |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Host**         | Iligan City                                         |
| **Main venue**   | Iligan Medical Center College covered court         |
| **Participants** | More than 650 women student-athletes                |
| **Schools**      | Delegates from over 15 universities across Mindanao |
| **Length**       | Five days, with championships on 4 June 2024        |
| **Tagline**      | "Kalaro. Kaibigan. Kasama."                         |

### Sports contested

- Basketball
- Volleyball
- Football
- Badminton
- Table tennis
- Obstacle race course
- Chess

### 2024 results

| Placing          | School                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overall champion | Palawan State University                                      |
| Second           | Saturnino Urios University                                    |
| Third            | Northwestern Mindanao State College of Science and Technology |

Alongside the competition, the 2024 edition ran a cultural night with the search for "Ms. MPG 2024", a "Kalaro. Kaibigan. Kasama." delegates' night, and forums with speakers from Spike and Serve, the Pilipinas Obstacle Sports Federation and SafeSport Philippines.

Coach Erwin Pelayo of Ateneo de Zamboanga University, one of the convenors, said the 2024 field was the largest since the games were inaugurated in 2014.

## How to take part

**The PSC does not publish an entry procedure, a registration form, a calendar, or contact details for the Mindanao Peace Games organisers.** Schools that want to field a delegation should approach the convening universities.

If you are an organiser seeking PSC support for an edition of the games, the relevant office is:

| Office                           | Phone                      | Email                      |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Assistance Coordination Division | 8525-0808 local 138 or 156 | supportservices@psc.gov.ph |

See [Request financial or equipment assistance](/services/partnerships-and-assistance/request-financial-or-equipment-assistance) for what the PSC does and does not publish about that process.
