# Batang Pinoy

Batang Pinoy is the Philippine Sports Commission's annual national youth games for athletes aged 15 and below. It was created by Executive Order No. 44 in 1998 and is deliberately built to include out-of-school youth and young people from marginalised communities, not only school teams.

---

## What Batang Pinoy is

Batang Pinoy is the baseline grassroots sports programme of the Philippine Sports Commission (PSC). It supports the national government's National Sports Development agenda by giving young athletes an inclusive competition to enter.

The programme's inclusive design is the point of it. Out-of-school youth, sectoral groups and athletes from marginalised sectors can compete in multiple sporting events alongside school-based athletes. PSC describes the games as centred on values formation: nationalism, self-discipline, respect, hard work, camaraderie and sportsmanship.

---

## Who can take part

|               |                                                                                                |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Age**       | 15 years old and below                                                                         |
| **Open to**   | School-based athletes, out-of-school youth, and athletes from sectoral and marginalised groups |
| **How often** | Annual                                                                                         |

The PSC does not publish a detailed eligibility rulebook, a residency rule, or an age cut-off date on its website. Those details are set for each edition.

---

## How the games began

| Year | What happened                                                                                                                        |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1998 | Executive Order No. 44 was signed by President Joseph Ejercito Estrada on 2 December, creating Batang Pinoy                          |
| 1999 | The games were formally inaugurated in Bacolod City, Negros Occidental, headed by then PSC Commissioner-in-Charge Monico Puentevella |

## How to enter

**The PSC does not publish an entry procedure, a qualifying calendar, a list of sports, or an entry form for Batang Pinoy on psc.gov.ph.** Entries are organised edition by edition, usually through host local governments and participating delegations.

The most current Batang Pinoy announcements are posted on the programme's official Facebook page:

- Batang Pinoy on Facebook: <https://www.facebook.com/BatangPinoyPSC>

If you are a local government, school or community group that wants to field a delegation, contact the PSC directly. PSC does not name the office that runs Batang Pinoy, but the two offices whose published remit is closest are:

| Office                                     | Phone                      | Email                      |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Program, Research and Development Division | 8525-0808 local 188 or 186 | prdd@psc.gov.ph            |
| Assistance Coordination Division           | 8525-0808 local 138 or 156 | supportservices@psc.gov.ph |
