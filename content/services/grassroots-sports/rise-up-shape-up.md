# Rise Up! Shape Up!

Rise Up! Shape Up! was a Philippine Sports Commission web series for women and girls, launched during the COVID-19 pandemic. It ran 64 episodes in its first 18 months and is part of the PSC's Women in Sports programme.

---

## What it was

Rise Up! Shape Up! was a digital show, not a live event or a facility-based class. The PSC produced it through the office of then Women in Sports oversight Commissioner Celia Kiram, releasing weekly webisodes aimed at Filipina viewers at a time when gyms, courts and playgrounds were closed.

The show discussed issues and topics affecting women and girls, and was open to viewers aged 7 and above.

## Who appeared on it

The series featured Filipina sports figures including:

- **Adeline Dumapong-Ancheta**, Paralympian
- **Akiko Thomson Guevara**, three-time Olympian
- **Cynthia Carrion**, president of the Gymnastics Association of the Philippines

---

## Is it still running?

**The PSC does not publish the current status of Rise Up! Shape Up!, an episode archive, or a link to where the episodes can be watched.** The most recent material the PSC publishes about the series dates from March 2024 and describes it in the past tense.
