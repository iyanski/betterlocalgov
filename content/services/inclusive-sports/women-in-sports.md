# Women in sports

Women in Sports is a standing Philippine Sports Commission programme with a designated oversight commissioner. It has three published components: an annual martial arts festival, an awards programme for grassroots women in sport, and a web series.

---

## The three components

| Component                                                                         | What it is                                                                          | Status                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Women's Martial Arts Festival**                                                 | An annual competition festival for Filipina martial artists, inaugurated April 2014 | Described by the PSC as annual                                   |
| **[Gintong Gawad Awards](/services/awards-and-recognition/gintong-gawad-awards)** | Awards honouring grassroots women in sport, in eight categories                     | Held in 2021 and 2022                                            |
| **[Rise Up! Shape Up!](/services/grassroots-sports/rise-up-shape-up)**            | A web series for women and girls, launched October 2020                             | 64 episodes in its first 18 months. Current status not published |

---

## Women's Martial Arts Festival

Filipina martial artists gather annually to compete across a wide range of martial arts. The PSC's stated purpose is to open sports usually seen as male-dominated to women and girls on equal terms.

|                   |                                                        |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Inaugurated**   | April 2014                                             |
| **Participation** | Over 15,000 women and girls have taken part since 2017 |

### Events contested

- Pencak silat
- Judo
- Karatedo
- Wrestling
- Muay thai
- Wushu
- Arnis
- Boxing
- Fencing
- Taekwondo

**The PSC does not publish the festival's dates, host venue, entry procedure, age divisions, weight classes, or results.** If you want to compete, ask the PSC and the national sports association for your martial art.

## Women's sport elsewhere in the PSC's work

The PSC's support for women's sport is not confined to this programme.

- The **[Mindanao Peace Games](/services/grassroots-sports/mindanao-peace-games)**, an all-women collegiate festival for over 650 student-athletes in 2024, received PSC support through the Office of Commissioner Olivia "Bong" Coo.
- Five of the nineteen Hall of Fame inductees the PSC profiles are women: Haydée Coloso-Espino, Lydia de Vega, Arianne Cerdeña, Elma Muros-Posadas and Gertrudes Lozada. See [Philippine Sports Hall of Fame](/services/awards-and-recognition/philippine-sports-hall-of-fame).

The PSC's mandate under Republic Act No. 6847, Section 6(b), is to encourage wide participation by all sectors, government and private, in amateur sports promotion and development.

---

## Oversight

Commissioner **Celia Kiram** held the Women in Sports oversight portfolio when Rise Up! Shape Up! and the Gintong Gawad Awards were created. Commissioner **Olivia "Bong" Coo** is named as women in sports oversight commissioner in the PSC's 2024 material, and also as oversight for coaches and athletes welfare.

**The PSC does not publish a current list of commissioner oversight assignments**, so confirm before relying on it.
