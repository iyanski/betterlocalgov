# Pilipinas ParaGames

The Pilipinas Para Games is the Philippine Sports Commission's grassroots sports programme for people with disabilities, run in partnership with the Philippine Paralympic Committee and with local governments. It grew out of the PSC's Differently-Abled Sport For Life programme, launched in 2018. The 2024 edition drew **860 athletes across nine sports**.

## How it started

In 2018 the PSC inaugurated **DAS4LIFE**, the Differently-Abled Sport For Life programme, to establish a sports development programme dedicated to people with disabilities. Through a partnership between then PSC Commissioner **Arnold Agustin** and the **Philippine Paralympic Committee (PPC)**, the programme was developed further, and the Pilipinas Para Games came out of it.

The stated purpose is to give people with disabilities, and children in particular, a safe space away from the influence of illegal drugs, and to build self-confidence and self-esteem.

## How it runs now

The games continue to be conducted and launched in provinces around the country. Each staging is run in collaboration with:

- The **local government unit**, through its **Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO)**
- The **Department of Education**, to bring in more children as participants

The PSC's aim for the programme is stated as national social change: mending divides that come from differences in individual skills and capabilities, and transcending language, physical and social barriers through sport.

**The PSC does not publish a schedule of upcoming legs, an entry procedure, eligibility or classification rules, a list of sports, or a hosting application.**

## Related national para sport

The **Philippine Paralympic Committee** is the body for Philippine para sport at national team level, and para sports associations fall under it. The PSC's 2024 Safe Sport Workshop invitations went to national sports associations including the sports under the Philippine Paralympic Committee.

The PSC has awarded incentives to para athletes after major Games, including the 2023 ASEAN Para Games and the 2023 Hangzhou Asian Para Games. See [Allowances and incentives](/services/national-athletes/athlete-allowances-and-incentives).
