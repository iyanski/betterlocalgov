# Host a PSC-backed event

Cities, provinces, schools and sports organisations have hosted Philippine Sports Commission competitions and PSC-supported events. There is no published bidding process, host selection criteria, or hosting application. This page sets out what hosting has looked like in practice and what you will need to establish directly with the PSC.

---

## What "PSC-backed" can mean

The PSC's involvement in an event takes more than one form, and it matters which one you are asking for.

| Type                                    | Example                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Who decides                                                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **A PSC programme staged in your area** | [Batang Pinoy](/services/grassroots-sports/batang-pinoy) inaugurated in Bacolod City in 1999. The [Indigenous Peoples Games](/services/grassroots-sports/indigenous-peoples-games) first leg in Tagum City, Davao del Norte, in 2018 | The PSC, with the host local government                                                          |
| **Support for an event you organise**   | The [Mindanao Peace Games](/services/grassroots-sports/mindanao-peace-games) in 2024, backed by the PSC after the convening universities requested it                                                                                | The PSC, on your request                                                                         |
| **Use of a PSC venue**                  | An event held at the Rizal Memorial Sports Complex or PhilSports                                                                                                                                                                     | Sports Facilities Division. See [Book a PSC venue](/services/sports-facilities/book-a-psc-venue) |

## What hosting has involved

The PSC does not publish host requirements, so this is drawn from how past editions were actually run.

| Element                           | What past hosts provided or arranged                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Venues**                        | Local competition venues, sometimes a single covered court, sometimes venues scattered across a city                                                              |
| **Local government backing**      | Provincial or city government support. The 2018 Indigenous Peoples Games leg was supported by the Province of Davao del Norte                                     |
| **Partner agencies**              | National agencies with a stake in the programme, such as the NCIP and NCCA for the Indigenous Peoples Games, or local PDAOs and DepEd for the Pilipinas ParaGames |
| **Accommodation and hospitality** | Delegations travel in. The 2024 Mindanao Peace Games drew over 650 athletes for a five-day event                                                                  |
| **A side programme**              | Cultural nights, forums and social events have formed part of past editions                                                                                       |

Treat this as a realistic picture of the commitment, not as an official requirements list.

---

## How to start the conversation

1. **Decide which of the three types above you are asking for.** Hosting a PSC programme, getting support for your own event, and renting a venue are different requests to different offices.
2. **Write to the office that fits.** Put the event, the dates, the venues, the expected number of participants, and what you are asking the PSC for in the letter.
3. **Ask for the requirements in writing.** None of them are published, so get them confirmed rather than assumed.
4. **Follow up by phone.** The PSC publishes no acknowledgement or turnaround commitment.

## Questions to get answered before you commit

Because nothing below is published, get each one confirmed in writing:

- What the PSC will provide, and what the host is expected to provide
- Whether any PSC funding attaches, and on what terms. See [Request financial or equipment assistance](/services/partnerships-and-assistance/request-financial-or-equipment-assistance)
- How far in advance a hosting request must be made
- Who signs on the PSC's behalf, and what document records the arrangement
- What branding, naming and reporting obligations the host takes on
- Who is liable for what, and what insurance the host must carry

| Information                                     | Status                       |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| A hosting application or bid form               | Does not exist on psc.gov.ph |
| Host selection criteria                         | Not published                |
| A bidding calendar for future editions          | Not published                |
| Host city requirements or technical manual      | Not published                |
| Cost-sharing terms between the PSC and the host | Not published                |
| A list of past and future host cities           | Not published                |
