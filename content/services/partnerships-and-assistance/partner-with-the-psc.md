# Partner with the PSC

Local governments, schools, national sports associations and private organisations all work with the Philippine Sports Commission. There is no online partnership portal and no published application process, so partnerships are arranged by writing to the right office. This page names those offices and sets out what the law lets the PSC do for a partner.

---

## Who the PSC partners with

Republic Act No. 6847, Section 7(a), names the sectors the PSC is required to coordinate with when it plans and implements the national sports programme:

- The Philippine Olympic Committee
- The national sports associations
- Public and private schools
- Government corporations and entities
- Local governments
- The Armed Forces of the Philippines
- Other sports organisations and private corporations

Section 6(b) makes this an objective in itself: to encourage wide participation of all sectors, government and private, in amateur sports promotion and development.

## What the PSC can do for a partner

The Commission's powers under Section 11 of RA 6847 include:

- **Enter into contracts** (Section 11(c))
- **Grant support or assistance to sports associations in good standing** with the Commission (Section 11(i))
- **Grant awards, benefits and privileges** to athletes, coaches and officials for outstanding performances (Section 11(h))
- **Accept donations, gifts, bequests and grants** for the purposes of the Commission (Section 11(k))
- **Call on any government entity for assistance** in performing its functions, with all heads of departments, agencies, corporations and government offices enjoined to render full assistance (Section 24)

### Tax treatment worth knowing

| Provision      | Effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Section 18** | No tax of any kind may be levied on the use of sports facilities, whether on the user, owner or operator                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Section 20** | Donations and contributions to the PSC for its fund-raising projects and continuing sports development programmes are exempt from donor's tax and fully deductible from the donor's taxable net income. The same applies to donations to the POC and to national sports associations that the PSC certifies are for sports development |
| **Section 19** | Sportswear, equipment, supplies and materials of international standard that are donated to the PSC, or donated through the PSC to national sports associations, are exempt from customs duties, taxes and tariffs where the conditions in that section are met                                                                        |

If your organisation is considering a donation, Section 20 is the provision to raise with your finance and tax advisers.

## For local governments

Section 7(m) of RA 6847 makes the PSC responsible for encouraging the creation of **regional, provincial, municipal and barangay or school district sports promotion and development councils**, composed of education and local government officials and private sector representatives, which initiate, conduct and coordinate sports activities in their own areas.

Section 7(j) also lets the PSC recommend to government agencies with sports programmes that they carry a separate and specific sports budget line.

**The PSC publishes no guide, model ordinance, or toolkit for setting up a local sports council.** Ask the Program, Research and Development Division what support is available.

| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |

| A list of current PSC partners                    | Not published on psc.gov.ph |

Assume you will need to write a letter, follow up by phone, and negotiate the terms directly.
