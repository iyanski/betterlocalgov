# Request financial or equipment assistance

The Philippine Sports Commission grants financial and equipment assistance to sports associations, teams and events. It publishes no application form, no criteria, and no documented process. This page tells you who to write to, what the law allows, and exactly which details you will have to ask for.

## What the PSC is empowered to give

Republic Act No. 6847 sets the boundaries.

| Provision         | What it allows                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Section 11(i)** | Confer, extend and grant support or assistance to sports associations **which are in good standing with the Commission**                                                                               |
| **Section 11(g)** | Assist and support national associations in implementing Section 13, which covers their competitions, training programmes and team selection                                                           |
| **Section 11(h)** | Confer, extend and grant awards, benefits and privileges to athletes, coaches and officials for outstanding performances in national and international competitions                                    |
| **Section 11(f)** | Regulate the acquisition, procurement, distribution and use of sportswear, equipment, instruments, tools and other sports necessities required for training the national pool of athletes              |
| **Section 7(i)**  | Provide incentives, recognition and awards to deserving associations, athletes, referees, game officials, coaches, trainers and other persons or entities involved in or supporting sports development |

Two things follow from this. First, **"good standing with the Commission" is the gate** for association assistance, and the PSC does not publish what good standing means or who currently has it. Second, equipment support for national pool training is a regulated PSC function, so equipment requests for national teams go through the PSC rather than around it.

## Where the money comes from

Section 26 of RA 6847 constitutes the **National Sports Development Fund**, which is automatically remitted to the Commission and made up of:

- 30 percent representing the charity fund and proceeds of six sweepstakes or lottery draws per year
- Taxes on horse races during special holidays
- 5 percent of the gross income of the Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation
- Proceeds from the sale of sports stamps printed by the Philippine Postal Service Office
- 3 percent of all taxes collected on imported athletic equipment

The same section requires that at least 80 percent of the PSC's annual appropriation, and **all** of the National Sports Development Fund, be disbursed for the national sports programme, particularly the identification, recruitment and training of athletes for pre-regional, regional, national and international competitions.

That is the fund your request is drawing on, and it is why assistance decisions are tied to the national sports programme rather than to general community spending.

## What is not published, and what to ask for

**There is no application form and no written procedure.** When you contact the Assistance Coordination Division, ask explicitly for each of the following, because none of it is on the website.

| Ask for                                                    | Why                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The current request format or template                     | So your letter is not rejected on form                                                                      |
| The documentary requirements                               | Registration papers, board resolutions, budgets and endorsements are likely, but the PSC does not list them |
| The eligibility criteria and what "good standing" requires | Section 11(i) makes this the deciding test                                                                  |
| The lead time before your event                            | So you submit early enough to be considered                                                                 |
| Who approves the request and at what level                 | Whether it is a division, the Executive Director, or the Commission                                         |
| The liquidation and reporting requirements if approved     | Public funds carry reporting duties                                                                         |
| Whether equipment is given in kind or as a cash grant      | This changes your own procurement planning                                                                  |

| Information                            | Status                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Application form                       | Does not exist on psc.gov.ph |
| Written step-by-step procedure         | Not published                |
| Eligibility criteria                   | Not published                |
| Deadlines or submission windows        | Not published                |
| Maximum grant size or funding ceilings | Not published                |
| Processing time                        | Not published                |
| Appeal route if refused                | Not published                |
| List of past grants and recipients     | Not published                |

## A related route: donations to sport

If you are on the giving side rather than the asking side, Section 20 of RA 6847 exempts donations and contributions to the PSC from donor's tax and makes them fully deductible from the donor's taxable net income. Donations to the Philippine Olympic Committee and to national sports associations, where the PSC certifies they are for sports development, are treated the same way. See [Partner with the PSC](/services/partnerships-and-assistance/partner-with-the-psc).
