# PhilHealth membership registration (informal economy members)

How the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) describes registration for informal economy members on its own website.

## Steps listed by PhilHealth

1. "Visit any of the Local Health Insurance Offices or PhilHealth Express outlets nationwide."
2. "Fill out (2) copies of the PhilHealth Member Registration Form (PMRF)"
3. "Submit PMRF to the LHIO or PhilHealth Express"
4. "Await Member Data Record (MDR) and PhilHealth ID Card"
5. "Pay the necessary premium contribution using your PhilHealth ID number"

PhilHealth lists monthly, quarterly, semi-annual and annual payment schedule options on the same page.

## What this page does not cover

Better Gattaran has not verified which PhilHealth office serves Gattaran, its location, hours or contact numbers. Registration rules differ for other member categories; see PhilHealth's own pages for those.
