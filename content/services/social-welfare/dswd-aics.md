# DSWD Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS)

The Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) describes AICS as "one of the Philippine government's initiatives" "to provide immediate and temporary assistance to individuals and families who are affected by crises."

## Types of assistance

DSWD says AICS "can help them meet their basic needs in the form of food, transportation, medical, educational and burial assistance."

## What the DSWD page does not state

The DSWD AICS page does not state eligibility rules, documentary requirements or how to apply. Better Gattaran has not verified any of these, nor which office handles AICS requests from Gattaran. The page lists a general inquiries email address, inquiry@dswd.gov.ph.

Confirm requirements and the application process with DSWD before relying on this summary.
