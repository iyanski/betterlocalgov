# Support for national coaches

National coaches are appointed by their national sports association, and the Philippine Sports Commission supports them through the same programmes it runs for athletes. The PSC publishes no coaching allowance rates, no accreditation scheme, and no coach development calendar.

---

## Who appoints national coaches

Under Republic Act No. 6847, Section 13(i), each national sports association selects "the athletes, coaches and other officials for their national teams". The NSA also adopts the training programme for its athletes, in coordination with the Philippine Olympic Committee, under Section 13(h).

The PSC does not hire national coaches. If you want to be considered as a national coach in your sport, approach the NSA for that sport. To find it, contact the **NSA Affairs Office** at nsaoo@psc.gov.ph.

---

## What the PSC's mandate covers for coaches

Republic Act No. 6847 names coaches explicitly in two places:

- **Section 7(i)**: the PSC provides incentives, recognition and awards to deserving associations, athletes, referees, game officials, **coaches**, trainers and other persons involved in or supporting sports development.
- **Section 11(h)**: the PSC may confer, extend and grant awards, benefits and privileges to athletes, **coaches** and officials for outstanding performances in national and international competitions.

**Republic Act No. 10699**, the National Athletes and Coaches Benefits and Incentives Act, is the governing law for coach benefits and incentives. The PSC does not publish its schedule, so no amounts are stated here.

## Programmes national coaches are included in

| Programme                   | What it covers                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Financial literacy seminars | The 2023 "Pera Mo, Kinabukasan Mo!" seminar was run for around 400 national training pool athletes **and coaches**. Earlier editions ran in 2015 and 2019               |
| Safe Sport Workshop         | The 2024 workshop asked every national sports association to send a board member or official, **a coach**, and an athlete                                               |
| Philippine Sports Institute | The PSC's institute for sports education and capability building. See [Seminars and capability building](/services/sports-development/seminars-and-capability-building) |

Commissioner Olivia "Bong" Coo has been the PSC's oversight commissioner for coaches and athletes welfare in the material the PSC publishes. The PSC does not publish a current list of commissioner oversight assignments, so confirm this before relying on it.

---

## What the PSC does not publish

| Information                                              | Status                      |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Coaching allowance or honorarium rates                   | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Coach incentive amounts for medals won by their athletes | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| A national coach accreditation or licensing scheme       | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| A coach development or certification calendar            | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Insurance or medical cover for national coaches          | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| How a coach raises a pay or welfare concern with the PSC | Not published on psc.gov.ph |

Note that licensing of referees, umpires and other game officials is an NSA function under Section 13(k) of RA 6847, not a PSC one.
