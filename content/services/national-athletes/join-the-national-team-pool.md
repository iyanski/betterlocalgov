# Join the national team pool

National athletes are selected by their national sports association, not by the Philippine Sports Commission. This page explains who decides, what the law says, and where to go if you want to be considered for a national team.

---

## Who selects national athletes

Under Republic Act No. 6847, the Philippine Sports Commission Act, each **national sports association (NSA)** is autonomous and holds exclusive technical control over its own sport. Section 13(i) makes selection an NSA function:

> To select the athletes, coaches and other officials for their national teams taking into consideration not only their athletic abilities but also their discipline, moral character, aptitude and attitude.

Section 13(h) adds that the NSA adopts the training programme for its athletes, in coordination with the Philippine Olympic Committee.

The PSC's role is different. It funds, supports and supervises. Section 11(j) gives the PSC supervisory and visitorial powers over NSAs in connection with the sports programmes it financially assists, and Section 26 directs the National Sports Development Fund towards the identification, recruitment and training of athletes for regional, national and international competition.

**So: if you want to be a national athlete in your sport, the organisation to approach is the NSA for that sport.**

---

## How to find the NSA for your sport

The PSC recognises NSAs that are organised for their sport in the Philippines or affiliated with the international federation recognised by the International Olympic Committee.

**The PSC does not publish a directory of recognised NSAs, their contact details, or their tryout calendars on psc.gov.ph.** To get the current list and a referral, contact:

| Office                                                 | Email            |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| NSA Affairs Office, Officer-in-Charge Liezl D. De Luna | nsaoo@psc.gov.ph |

The Philippine Olympic Committee, recognised in Section 12 of RA 6847 as the country's National Olympic Committee, is the other place to ask. Each NSA appoints its own representative to the POC.

## Routes that lead towards the pool

The PSC's grassroots competitions exist partly to surface talent. They are open entry points that do not require you to already be on a national team.

| Programme                                                                          | Who it is for                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Batang Pinoy](/services/grassroots-sports/batang-pinoy)                           | Athletes aged 15 and below, including out-of-school youth                       |
| [Philippine National Games](/services/grassroots-sports/philippine-national-games) | Listed as a PSC national competition, though no details are currently published |
| [Pilipinas ParaGames](/services/inclusive-sports/pilipinas-paragames)              | Athletes with disabilities                                                      |
| [Indigenous Peoples Games](/services/grassroots-sports/indigenous-peoples-games)   | Athletes from indigenous communities                                            |

## What the PSC does not publish

| Information                                                   | Status        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| A national team tryout calendar                               | Not published |
| Application or nomination forms for the training pool         | Not published |
| Qualifying standards or selection criteria by sport           | Not published |
| The list of recognised national sports associations           | Not published |
| The size or composition of the current national training pool | Not published |

The one athlete list the PSC does publish is the masterlist of national team members qualified for the 20 percent discount privilege. See [Allowances and incentives](/services/national-athletes/athlete-allowances-and-incentives).
