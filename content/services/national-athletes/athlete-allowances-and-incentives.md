# Allowances and incentives for national athletes

The only benefit the Philippine Sports Commission currently publishes for national athletes is a 20 percent discount privilege on goods and services. Allowance rates and medal incentive amounts are not published on psc.gov.ph, and this page will not invent them.

---

## What is published: the 20 percent discount privilege

The PSC maintains a masterlist of qualified national team members entitled to a **20 percent discount privilege on goods and services**.

|                        |                                                                      |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Benefit**            | 20 percent discount privilege on goods and services                  |
| **Who qualifies**      | National team members named on the PSC masterlist                    |
| **Current masterlist** | List of qualified athletes as of 7 October 2025                      |
| **Where to check**     | The masterlist is published as a downloadable PDF on the PSC website |

If you are a national athlete, check whether your name appears on the current masterlist. If it does not and you believe it should, raise it with your national sports association first, since NSAs submit their team rosters, and then with the PSC.

---

## The governing law on incentives

**Republic Act No. 10699**, the National Athletes and Coaches Benefits and Incentives Act, is the law that governs cash incentives and benefits for national athletes and coaches. It is the statute that sets out what medallists are entitled to.

The PSC does not reproduce RA 10699's incentive schedule on its website. **This page therefore names the law but states no amounts, no medal-by-medal rates, and no payment timelines.** For the actual figures, read RA 10699 itself or ask the PSC.

Republic Act No. 6847, the PSC Act, is the separate statute that gives the PSC the power to grant these benefits at all:

- **Section 7(i)**: the PSC provides incentives, recognition and awards to deserving associations, athletes, referees, game officials, coaches, trainers and other persons or entities involved in sports development.
- **Section 11(h)**: the PSC may confer, extend and grant awards, benefits and privileges to athletes, coaches and officials for outstanding performances in national and international competitions.

## Incentives are paid, even though the rates are not on the website

The PSC's own records show incentive awarding ceremonies for recent international campaigns:

| Date            | Event                                                                     |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 11 July 2023    | Awarding of incentives for 2023 SEA Games and ASEAN Para Games medallists |
| 27 October 2023 | Awarding of incentives to the 2023 Hangzhou Asian Games medallists        |
| 27 January 2024 | Awarding of incentives for 2023 Hangzhou Asian Para Games medallists      |

So incentives for medallists are real and are paid after major Games. What is missing from the website is the schedule that would let an athlete work out what they are owed and when.
