# Welfare, insurance, and benefits

Athlete welfare is a stated priority of the Philippine Sports Commission, and the PSC runs real welfare work including medical services, housing, safeguarding and financial education. What it does not publish is an insurance policy, a benefits schedule, or a claims process. This page separates the two.

## The one published benefit

The single athlete benefit the PSC names on its website is a **20 percent discount privilege on goods and services**, available to national team members on the PSC masterlist. See [Allowances and incentives](/services/national-athletes/athlete-allowances-and-incentives).

**Republic Act No. 10699**, the National Athletes and Coaches Benefits and Incentives Act, is the governing law for athlete benefits and incentives. The PSC does not reproduce its provisions on the website, so this page names the law without quoting entitlements from it.

## Financial literacy for athletes

The PSC has run financial literacy seminars for national athletes and coaches three times: in 2015, in 2019, and in September 2023.

The 2023 edition, "Pera Mo, Kinabukasan Mo!", was held at the Rizal Memorial Coliseum for around 400 national training pool athletes and coaches. It was overseen by Commissioner Walter Torres and brought in speakers from Landbank of the Philippines and the Overseas Filipino Bank, Pioneer Life, Scam Watch Pilipinas, Pag-IBIG and TESDA, alongside financial educator Chinkee Tan.

The PSC does not publish a schedule for future editions.

## What the PSC does not publish

This is the part athletes most often need and cannot find.

| Information                                                            | Status                      |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Whether national athletes are covered by insurance, and by what policy | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Medical coverage limits or what treatment is paid for                  | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| How to make a medical or injury claim                                  | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Injury and rehabilitation support for athletes hurt in training        | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Retirement, pension or post-career benefits                            | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Benefits for retired or former national athletes                       | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| A written athlete welfare policy                                       | Not published on psc.gov.ph |

If you are a national athlete who needs any of the above, do not assume the cover exists. Confirm it in writing with your national sports association and with the PSC before you need it.
