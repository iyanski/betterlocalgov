# Education and scholarship support

The Philippine Sports Commission runs no published scholarship programme for national athletes. What it has worked on is a route into government employment after an athlete's competing career, through a proposed civil service eligibility grant with the Civil Service Commission. This page sets out where that stands.

---

## Civil service eligibility for national athletes

The Civil Service Commission (CSC) and the PSC have discussed granting **government service eligibility to national athletes who have won medals in international games**, so that athletes can enter government service after their sporting careers.

|                            |                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **First proposed**         | February 2020, by the CSC to the PSC                                                                              |
| **Revisited**              | February 2023, during a CSC courtesy visit to the Rizal Memorial Sports Complex                                   |
| **Legal basis being used** | Republic Act No. 6847 (the PSC Act) and Republic Act No. 2260 (the Civil Service Act of 1959)                     |
| **Status**                 | A plan under discussion. The PSC publishes no signed agreement, no implementing rules, and no application process |

CSC Commissioner Atty. Aileen Lizada put the case for it this way in 2023: "the current examinations for eligibility would not totally capture the skill set of our national athletes. That is why we are pushing for this special grant so that they may still be in government service after their careers and help them ensure their future."

### What the two eligibility levels mean

Under the Civil Service Act, the grant being discussed would map onto existing eligibility levels:

| Eligibility          | What it qualifies you for                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Sub-Professional** | First level positions such as clerical, trade and custodial service posts, which require less than four years of college education   |
| **Professional**     | First and second level positions such as professional, technical and scientific posts, which require four years of college education |

**Do not treat this as an available benefit.** The PSC has published nothing since 2023 to say the grant now exists. If you are a medallist, check the current position with the Civil Service Commission directly before making plans around it.

## The governing law on education benefits

**Republic Act No. 10699**, the National Athletes and Coaches Benefits and Incentives Act, is the statute that covers education and other benefits for national athletes and coaches. The PSC does not reproduce its provisions, so this page names the law without quoting entitlements, tuition subsidies, or slot counts from it.

## What the PSC does not publish

| Information                                                         | Status                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| A PSC scholarship programme for athletes                            | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Tuition assistance or study grants                                  | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Partner schools or universities                                     | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Application forms, deadlines or eligibility rules for study support | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| Study leave arrangements for athletes in the national pool          | Not published on psc.gov.ph |
| The status of the CSC eligibility grant after 2023                  | Not published on psc.gov.ph |

If a scholarship is offered to you in the PSC's name, verify it with the PSC before paying anything or signing anything.
